{% comment %} Toast region - included once in base.html. Driven by Alpine $store.toasts (static/js/app.js). Announcing and showing are separate: - The two sr-only live regions exist at page load (a live region added to the DOM together with its content is not reliably announced). The store writes each message into one of them exactly once: assertive for a failure, polite otherwise. - The visible stack is not a live region, so re-rendering it (another toast arriving, a timer pausing) never re-reads a message. data-dialog-exempt keeps this region out of the inert background while a dialog is open, so "Saved" raised from inside a modal is still seen and heard. Failures stay until dismissed; hovering or focusing a toast pauses its timer. {% endcomment %}
