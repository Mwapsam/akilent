AI suggestions

When a customer writes and none of your automations answered, AI can draft a reply for your team. AI never sends anything. A person on your team reads the suggestion, edits it if needed, and presses Send. Everything else in Akilent works the same with AI switched off.

What is sent to the AI service

  • The last few messages of the one conversation being answered. Phone numbers and email addresses in them are hidden.
  • The customer's first name and tags, and whether they're tracked as interested.
  • Your business name, opening hours, approved templates and the notes below.

Internal notes, other customers and other conversations are never sent.
