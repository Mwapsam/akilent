AI suggestions

When a customer writes and none of your automations answered, AI can draft a reply for your team. A person on your team reads the suggestion, edits it if needed, and presses Send. AI sends nothing on its own unless you choose automatic replies below, and then only for the kinds of question you tick. Everything else in Akilent works the same with AI switched off.

What is sent to the AI service

  • The last few messages of the one conversation being answered, and a short summary of its earlier messages. Phone numbers and email addresses in them are hidden.
  • The customer's first name and tags, whether they're tracked as interested, and, when AI asks, their open follow-up and last few orders.
  • Your business name, opening hours, approved templates, tag names and the notes below, and, when AI asks, matching products and prices from your catalogue.

Internal notes, other customers and other conversations are never sent.
