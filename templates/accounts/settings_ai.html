Sent once, then AI stays quiet until someone on your team replies.

AI still hands it to your team when:

  • a price isn't in your catalogue or notes, a time isn't one of your opening hours, or a number or link can't be checked (so it can't invent a price or date);
  • a teammate has taken the conversation or replied in the last few minutes;
  • the 24-hour window has closed, or a template is needed;
  • the customer has a complaint, wants a refund or a cancellation, or disputes something;
  • the answer isn't in your knowledge base or notes;
  • it has already answered 5 times in a row without your team.

Replies AI sends are marked “Sent by AI” in the inbox. The autopilot report shows every reply it sent or held back, and why.

What is sent to the AI service

Only what's needed to draft one reply.

  • The last few messages of the one conversation being answered, and a short summary of its earlier messages. Phone numbers and email addresses in them are hidden.
  • The customer's first name and tags, whether they're tracked as interested, and, when AI asks, their open follow-up and last few orders.
  • Your business name, opening hours, approved templates, tag names, the notes below, your knowledge base entries (the ones closest to the customer's question when you have many), and products and prices from your catalogue.

Internal notes, other customers and other conversations are never sent.
