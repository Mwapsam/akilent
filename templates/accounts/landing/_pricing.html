{% comment %} Pricing for a global buyer. Cards, "All plans include" and the comparison table come from billing_api.pricing_table and plan_card, read from the plans themselves, so what's shown is what each plan gives. Prices go through billing's money filter, in BILLING_CURRENCY. Layout: the free plan isn't a card but a line above the cards (a way to start, not a plan to compare), beside the Monthly / Annual switch. Every card has the same structure: name and who it's for, price with a reserved line for the other term, the button straight under the price, then what it adds to the plan before ("Everything in Starter, plus:"). Professional is the one tinted card with the one amber button; Enterprise is the dark card that ends the row. Monthly / annual: two radios before the cards; the one checked hides the other term's price, note and button with a sibling selector, so the switch works without JavaScript. The annual price leads per month (pricing.monthly_equivalent, rounded) with the exact yearly total checkout charges under it (pricing.price_for), and its button opens signup with ?period=annually. Enterprise lists only what can be set up on request today (custom limits and features for one workspace, annual billing, a walkthrough). Multiple numbers, invoicing and guided onboarding stay off until they exist. {% endcomment %} {% load billing_money %}
