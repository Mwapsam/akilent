How do you know which post brought a sale?

Each post can carry its own WhatsApp chat link. When a customer comes through that link, or through a reply to a comment on the post, the chat is linked to the post, and so are the leads and orders that follow. Customers who found you another way aren't counted, so the real total can be higher.

WhatsApp

Can I bring my existing WhatsApp number?

Yes. You connect the number you already use through Meta's guided setup, and your customers keep messaging the same number. Once connected it works through {{ app }} instead of the WhatsApp Business app, so a number that's in the app today moves out of it.

Do I need to sign up for the WhatsApp Business API myself?

No. During setup you connect your business number through Meta's guided sign-in, and {{ app }} handles the rest. The number you connect is used through {{ app }} rather than the WhatsApp Business app.

Can my team share one number?

Yes. Everyone works from the same inbox, and you can assign each conversation to a teammate so customers aren't answered twice or missed.

Who pays WhatsApp's message fees?

Meta bills them directly to your WhatsApp Business account, at Meta's rates for each country. They aren't part of your {{ app }} plan, and {{ app }} never adds a markup. See Meta's pricing (opens in a new tab).

Does AI send messages automatically?

Not unless you allow it. AI is off until you switch it on, and then it suggests replies for your team to review and send. You can choose to let it answer a few simple questions, such as your opening hours, on its own. Every reply it sends is marked and logged.

Plans and billing

What happens after the trial?

Nothing is deleted. Your customers, conversations and settings stay in your account. Choose a plan to keep sending messages and using your plan's features.

Do you offer annual billing or invoices?

Yes to annual billing: choose Annually on any paid plan{% if pricing.annual_save_pct %} and save {{ pricing.annual_save_pct }}%{% endif %}. Every payment is confirmed by email, with the plan and the amount, for your records. Invoices against a purchase order aren't available yet.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. An owner or admin can cancel from the Billing page at any time. Your plan stays on until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
