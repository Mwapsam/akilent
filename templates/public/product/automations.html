Good intentions don't follow up

A customer asks a price at 9pm and hears nothing until morning. Someone promises to check back on Friday and forgets. A paid order waits for a thank-you that never goes out. None of it is anyone's fault: it just depends on people remembering.

What runs for you

Replies when you can't

Greet new customers, and send a reply when you're closed that knows your opening hours, so nobody is left wondering.

Answers by keyword

When a message contains a word you choose, such as "price" or "delivery", send the answer you've set.

Follow-up reminders

Set a follow-up on a conversation, and your team is told when it's due.

Questions about automations

Can I send a different reply when we're closed?

Yes. Set your opening hours, and the closed-hours reply goes out only outside them.

How do I know an automation does what I expect?

Test it first. You see every step it would take for a pretend customer, and nothing is sent or saved.

Does {{ app }} message customers to follow up for me?

Follow-ups and nudges remind your team. {{ app }} only messages a customer through an automatic reply or workflow you set up.
