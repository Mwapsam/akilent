The same ten questions, all day

Prices, delivery, opening hours, where you are, whether something is in stock. Most messages ask something you've answered a hundred times, and each one still takes a person to type it out again.

How it works

1. Teach it your answers

Add answers one by one, import a list, or have {{ app }} read them from your website. Imported and website answers wait for your review before AI uses them.

2. It suggests, your team sends

In the inbox, AI suggests a reply from your answers. Your team reviews it, edits it if needed and sends it.

3. Simple questions on its own, if you allow it

You can let AI answer a few simple questions, such as your opening hours, on its own. Every reply it sends is marked and logged.

Questions about AI answers

Does AI send messages automatically?

Not unless you allow it. AI is off until you switch it on, and then it suggests replies for your team to review and send. You can choose to let it answer a few simple questions, such as your opening hours, on its own. Every reply it sends is marked and logged.

Where does AI get its answers?

From your business information: answers you add, a list you import or pages read from your website.
