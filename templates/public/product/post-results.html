Likes don't pay the bills

Reach and likes tell you a post was seen. They don't tell you whether anyone bought. Without that, it's hard to know which posts to make more of, and which to stop.

How a post earns its credit

  • Each post can carry its own WhatsApp chat link.
  • When a customer comes through that link, or through a reply to a comment on the post, the chat is linked to the post.
  • The leads and orders that follow are credited to the same post, fixed when they're created.
  • The post shows its clicks, chats, leads, orders and paid revenue, per channel.

Only explicit evidence counts. Customers who found you another way aren't counted, so the real total can be higher.

Next to what the platforms report

Beside the sales numbers, see each post's reach and engagement from Instagram and Facebook, so you can compare what got attention with what got customers.

For the business as a whole, Business Health shows what your conversations turned into: replies, follow-ups and sales.

Questions about post results

How do you know which post brought a sale?

Each post can carry its own WhatsApp chat link. When a customer comes through that link, or through a reply to a comment on the post, the chat is linked to the post, and so are the leads and orders that follow. Customers who found you another way aren't counted, so the real total can be higher.

Where does the revenue figure come from?

From the orders your team records and marks paid in {{ app }} when the customer pays you.
