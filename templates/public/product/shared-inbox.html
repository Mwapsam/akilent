Questions about the shared inbox

Which channels arrive in the inbox?

{{ chat_names }} messages arrive in one shared inbox.{% if ch.email == "live" %} Email is for sending messages and campaigns from your own domain; replies to those emails don't come into the inbox.{% endif %}

{% if ch.whatsapp == "live" %}
Can my team share one number?

Yes. Everyone works from the same inbox, and you can assign each conversation to a teammate so customers aren't answered twice or missed. Teammates join as Owner, Admin or Member.

{% endif %}
Does AI reply to customers on its own?

Not unless you allow it. AI is off until you switch it on, and then it suggests replies for your team to review and send. You can choose to let it answer a few simple questions on its own. Every reply it sends is marked and logged.
