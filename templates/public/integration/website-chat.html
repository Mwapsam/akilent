Questions about website chat

What happens when the chatbot can't answer?

It tells the visitor that someone from your team will reply, and the chat goes to your shared inbox with a note saying why. Your team is emailed, and their reply appears in the visitor's chat.

Does it make things up?

It answers only from your own information, with the same checks as AI replies on your other channels. When it isn't sure, it hands the chat to your team instead of guessing.

Is website chat in every plan?

It depends on your plan. Ask us if yours doesn't show Chatbots.
