This policy explains what personal data {{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }} ("we") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the businesses that use Akilent, their team members, and the customers who message those businesses. {{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }} is operated by [COMPANY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Zambia.

Who is responsible for what

  • Your account data (your name, email, business details, billing): we decide how it's used, so we are the controller.
  • Your customers' data (their WhatsApp messages, phone numbers, names, orders): the business decides how it's used and we process it on the business's behalf, so the business is the controller and we are the processor. If you are a customer of a business that uses Akilent, contact that business first about your data. You can also contact us.

What we collect

  • Account information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, business name and profile (what you sell, location, opening hours, payment methods), and team roles.
  • Conversations: WhatsApp messages sent to and from the business, with media, delivery status, and the customer's phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • Business records: contacts, tags, leads, deals, orders and payments the business records in Akilent.
  • Usage and technical data: log-in times, IP addresses, browser type, and error reports, used to keep the service secure and working.
  • Billing: plan and payment status. Card and mobile-money details are handled by our payment providers; we don't store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To run the service: deliver messages, show conversations in the inbox, run the automations a business turns on, and produce its reports.
  • To support you, keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To bill for the service and meet legal obligations.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use a business's customer data for our own marketing.

AI features

AI features are off unless a business turns them on. When a business opts in, parts of a conversation (recent messages and the business's own notes) are sent to an AI provider to suggest replies or help set up automations. Before sending, we replace phone numbers and email addresses in the text with placeholders. The AI provider returns a suggestion; a person at the business decides what is sent unless the business has chosen automatic replies for specific topics. Who we share data with

We use these service providers (sub-processors) to run Akilent, each only for its purpose:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform): sending and receiving WhatsApp messages. Meta's own terms and privacy policy also apply to WhatsApp.
  • Amazon Web Services: file storage, email delivery and backups.
  • [HOSTING PROVIDER]: the servers that run Akilent, located in [HOSTING LOCATION].
  • Flutterwave and Stripe: payments.
  • Sentry: error reports (configured not to include personal data where possible).
  • AI providers (only for businesses that turn AI on): [AI PROVIDERS IN USE, e.g. Ollama, Anthropic, OpenAI].

Some of these providers process data outside Zambia. Where they do, we rely on their contractual safeguards.

We also disclose data when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Conversation and business records: for as long as the business keeps its account, or until the business deletes them.
  • After a business closes its account, we delete its data within [90] days, except what we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.
  • Backups are kept for 30 days and then deleted.
  • One-time codes sent for another system are deleted from our records as soon as they have been sent.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. WhatsApp access tokens are stored encrypted. Backups are encrypted and private. Team access is limited by role.

Your rights

Under Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how it's used, and ask for a copy of it. See Data deletion for how to ask us to delete data. You can also complain to Zambia's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell account owners by email before the change takes effect.
