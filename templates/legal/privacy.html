This Privacy Policy explains how {{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }} ("Akilent", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects personal data. It applies to businesses that use Akilent, their team members, and customers who communicate with those businesses through Akilent.

Akilent is operated by Progstack Solutions, a company registered in Zambia, with its registered office at LIVINGSTONE RD, LUS 10/13, KABWATA, LUSAKA.

Who is responsible for what

Akilent handles different types of data in different roles.

  • Business account data. For account information such as your name, email address, business details, subscription and billing information, Akilent acts as the data controller.
  • Customer data. For customer conversations, contacts, orders and other business records stored in Akilent, the business using Akilent is the data controller and Akilent acts as a data processor, processing that information only on the business's instructions.

If you are a customer of a business that uses Akilent, you should normally contact that business first about requests relating to your personal data. You may also contact us if you believe we can help direct your request.

What we collect

  • Account information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, business name, industry, location, opening hours, payment methods, subscription plan, and team member roles.
  • Conversations: WhatsApp messages sent to and from the business, including media attachments, timestamps, delivery status, customer phone numbers and WhatsApp profile names.
  • Business records: contacts, tags, conversation labels, leads, deals, orders, invoices, payments and notes created by the business.
  • Usage and technical data: login activity, IP addresses, browser type, device information, audit logs and error reports used to operate and secure the service.
  • Billing information: subscription status, invoices and payment confirmations. Payment card and mobile-money details are processed by our payment providers. We do not store full payment card numbers.

How we use personal data

We use personal data only for legitimate purposes connected with providing Akilent.

  • Deliver and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Display conversations in the shared inbox.
  • Run automations that a business enables.
  • Generate analytics and reports for the business.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Protect accounts against fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • Maintain, improve and troubleshoot the service.
  • Process subscriptions and payments.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

We do not use a business's customer conversations or customer records for our own advertising or marketing.

AI features

AI features are disabled by default. A business must choose to enable them.

When enabled, Akilent may send limited conversation context—such as recent messages and the business's own notes—to an AI provider to generate reply suggestions or help configure automations.

Before sending text where possible, Akilent replaces phone numbers and email addresses with placeholders to reduce unnecessary personal data exposure. The AI provider returns a suggested response, which remains under the business's control unless the business has explicitly configured automatic replies for specific approved workflows.

AI providers process this information only to provide the requested AI functionality and not to market directly to the business's customers through Akilent.

International transfers

Because some of our service providers operate globally, personal data may be processed in countries outside Zambia. We take reasonable steps to ensure these providers protect personal data through contractual commitments, security controls and other appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep data

  • Conversation history, contacts and business records remain available while the business keeps them.
  • When a business deletes records, they are removed from active systems as part of our deletion process.
  • After an account is permanently closed, we delete remaining business data within 90 days, except where we must retain certain records for legal, regulatory or accounting purposes.
  • Encrypted backups are retained for up to 30 days before being automatically deleted.
  • Temporary one-time verification codes are retained only as long as necessary to complete delivery and verification.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS);
  • encrypted storage of sensitive credentials such as WhatsApp access tokens;
  • encrypted backups;
  • role-based access controls;
  • security logging and monitoring;
  • regular software updates and security maintenance; and
  • data hosting on Amazon Web Services (AWS EC2) infrastructure.

While no online service can guarantee absolute security, we work to reduce risks through appropriate safeguards and operational practices.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021, you may have the right to:

  • request access to your personal data;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of personal data where applicable;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request restriction of processing; and
  • request a copy of personal data in an appropriate format where applicable.

You can submit requests through our Data Deletion page or by contacting us.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you may also lodge a complaint with Zambia's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children's privacy

Akilent is intended for businesses and is not designed for children to create accounts directly. Businesses remain responsible for ensuring they have an appropriate legal basis for processing customer information through Akilent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify account owners by email or through the Akilent dashboard before those changes take effect where reasonably possible.
