This page explains how to request deletion of your personal data from {{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }}. It also serves as Akilent's data deletion instructions for Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

If you messaged a business that uses Akilent

  1. Contact the business and ask it to delete your conversation and contact details. The business controls this data and can instruct us to delete it.
  2. Or email support@akilent.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include the WhatsApp number you used and the name of the business you contacted. We will verify the request with the business and process deletion within 30 days where applicable.

Replying STOP to a business stops future messages from that business, but it does not delete previous messages.

Deleting data from Akilent does not remove copies stored on your own device or within WhatsApp's systems. Those are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy practices.

If you have an Akilent account

  1. To delete a customer's contact details and conversations, the account owner should email support@akilent.com with the customer's WhatsApp number.
  2. To close an Akilent account and request deletion of all business data, the account owner should email support@akilent.com from the account owner's registered email address.
  3. We delete active account data within 90 days of confirmed account closure, except records we are legally required to retain, such as invoices or accounting records. Encrypted backups containing deleted data expire automatically within 30 days.

Disconnecting WhatsApp

If you remove Akilent's access through Meta Business Settings, Akilent will stop sending and receiving WhatsApp messages for that connection.

Existing data already stored in Akilent remains until it is deleted using one of the methods described above.

Questions

If you have questions about data deletion, email support@akilent.com. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
