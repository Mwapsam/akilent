This page explains how to request deletion of your personal data from {{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }}. It also serves as Akilent's data deletion instructions for Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Instagram Platform.

If you messaged a business that uses Akilent

  1. Contact the business and ask it to delete your conversation and contact details. The business controls this data and can instruct us to delete it.
  2. Or email support@akilent.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include the WhatsApp number or Instagram username you used and the name of the business you contacted. We will verify the request with the business and process deletion within 30 days where applicable.

On WhatsApp, replying STOP to a business stops future messages from that business, but it does not delete previous messages.

Deleting data from Akilent does not remove copies stored on your own device or within WhatsApp's or Instagram's systems. Those are governed by Meta's own privacy practices.

If you have an Akilent account

  1. To delete a customer's contact details and conversations, the account owner or an admin opens the customer in Customers and chooses More → Delete customer. This removes their record, conversations, messages, orders, notes and their WhatsApp and Instagram details straight away; several customers can be deleted at once from the Customers list. You can also email support@akilent.com with the customer's WhatsApp number or Instagram username and we will do it for you.
  2. To close an Akilent account and request deletion of all business data, the account owner should email support@akilent.com from the account owner's registered email address.
  3. We delete active account data within 90 days of confirmed account closure, except records we are legally required to retain, such as invoices or accounting records. Encrypted backups containing deleted data expire automatically within 30 days.

Disconnecting WhatsApp

If you remove Akilent's access through Meta Business Settings, Akilent will stop sending and receiving WhatsApp messages for that connection.

Existing data already stored in Akilent remains until it is deleted using one of the methods described above.

Disconnecting Instagram

You can disconnect an Instagram account from Settings → Instagram in Akilent, or by removing Akilent under Apps and websites in your Instagram settings. Either way, Akilent stops receiving and sending Instagram messages and comments for that account. Disconnecting keeps the conversation history in Akilent so the business can still see it.

To have that data deleted as well, use Remove and request deletion in your Instagram settings under Apps and websites, or email support@akilent.com. Akilent then deletes what it received through that Instagram account: its direct messages, comments, attachments and the Instagram usernames and names of the people who wrote to it. Contact records the business also uses on another channel, or for its own orders and deals, keep those other details.

Questions

If you have questions about data deletion, email support@akilent.com. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
