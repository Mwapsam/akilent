This page explains what cookies and similar technologies Akilent uses, why we use them, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember information about your visit, such as your login session or preferences.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the application to function. You cannot opt out of them.

  • Session cookie — keeps you logged in during your session. Expires when your browser closes or after 8 hours, whichever comes first. Set on the akilent.com domain, HttpOnly and Secure.
  • CSRF token — protects form submissions against cross-site request forgery attacks. Set on the akilent.com domain.
  • analytics_consent — remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, for one year.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics to see how our website is used, but only if you click Accept in the cookie bar. Until you do, and if you decline, Google Analytics isn't loaded and none of these cookies are set.

  • _ga, _ga_*, _gid — Google Analytics audience measurement. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Performance monitoring

We use Cloudflare Insights to measure page load performance. Cloudflare may set cookies or use local storage for this purpose. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

How to control cookies

To change your analytics choice, open the cookie bar again and pick Accept or Decline. Declining stops new analytics cookies; your browser settings (below) remove ones already set.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in.
