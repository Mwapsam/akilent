This page sets out how {{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }} protects your business's data today. It lists only what is in place now. For what data we collect and why, see the privacy policy.

Hosting

{{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }} runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS). The application runs on AWS EC2, files and backups are stored in Amazon S3, and static files are served through Amazon CloudFront. Cloudflare sits in front of akilent.com.

Encryption

  • In transit: every connection uses HTTPS (TLS). Browsers are told to use HTTPS only, for a year (HSTS, preloaded).
  • At rest: the production database disk is encrypted (AWS EBS encryption), and backups are encrypted in Amazon S3.
  • Access tokens: the tokens that connect your WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook accounts are also encrypted field by field in the database.

Access control

  • Every teammate signs in with their own account and a role: Owner, Admin or Member.
  • Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, an email code or a passkey, with recovery codes for when a device is lost.
  • When {{ site.app_name|default:"Akilent" }} support looks at your workspace to help you, the view is read-only: nothing can be sent, saved or changed. It ends after an hour, and each view is recorded in the audit log.

Audit log

Sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log: sign-ins and failed sign-ins, password and two-step sign-in changes, passkeys and recovery codes, team invitations and role changes, API keys, contact deletions and merges, and WhatsApp campaign sends. Entries are never edited or deleted.

Backups and recovery

The database is backed up every night to a private, encrypted Amazon S3 bucket. Backups are kept for 30 days, and every week the newest one is restored into a separate database to check that it works.

Incidents

We keep written runbooks for outages and security incidents. If a security incident affects your data, we tell you directly.

Your data

  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time: see data deletion.
  • AI is off until you switch it on.
