A website chatbot puts a chat button on your website. It greets visitors, answers their questions from your Knowledge, captures leads, and hands the chat to your team when a person is needed. Every chat lands in your inbox like your other conversations. Set it up from Chatbots.

Create a chatbot

  1. Click New chatbot, give it a name (visitors see it at the top of the chat) and add your website address.
  2. Pick how it should start:
    • Answer questions, hand over when stuck: AI answers each question from your Knowledge. When it isn't sure, it tells the visitor a person will reply and lets your team know.
    • Capture sales leads: greets visitors with two buttons. Anyone who wants a quote is tracked as interested, asked what they need and how to reach them, and your team is told.
    • Help choose a product: looks up the product a visitor asks about in your catalogue and gives its price. Anything else is answered by AI, or handed to your team.

The chatbot's page then shows a Get it live checklist. Nothing reaches visitors until you turn the conversation on.

Try it before visitors do

  • Click Try it to chat as a visitor would. It uses your latest saved changes, even ones that aren't live yet, and nothing is sent to anyone or saved.
  • Tick Show what happens behind the scenes to see each decision: which button was chosen, that a lead would be created, who would be emailed.
  • Where the real chatbot's AI would answer, the preview says so instead of answering, so trying it never uses your AI allowance.
  • To change the wording or the steps, click Edit messages and steps. That opens the conversation in the automation editor, where it works like any other automation. Save, then try it again.

Turn it on

On the chatbot's page, click Turn it on. Only an owner or admin can. After you change it later, the button says Put changes live; visitors already in a chat finish the way they started.

A chatbot can also run without a conversation of its own (in Settings, choose None: AI answers every question). It then answers every message from your Knowledge, and hands over when it can't.

Install it on your website

  1. Copy the code under Install on your website.
  2. Paste it before the closing </body> tag on every page that should show the chat. Website builders call this "custom code", "footer code" or "code injection".
  3. Open your website. The chat button appears in the corner.

The chat only loads on the websites in the chatbot's list. Each one shows Working once the chat has loaded there. If the chat tried to load on an address that isn't in the list (often www. versus no www.), the page names it and offers Add this website.

How it answers

  • The chatbot's conversation runs first: its buttons, questions and steps.
  • When the conversation doesn't answer a message, AI answers from your Knowledge only, with the same checks as automatic replies on your other channels. If AI is off for your business, chats go straight to your team.
  • AI never takes actions on its own. Leads, notifications and assignments happen only through steps you put in the conversation.
  • Each chatbot has a daily AI limit (200 a day unless you change it in Settings). When it's reached, chats go to your team until the next day. Your plan's AI allowance applies as well.

When a visitor needs a person

  • Visitors can tap Talk to a person at the top of the chat, or simply ask ("can I speak to someone?"). The chatbot stops answering in that chat, and the visitor is told a person will reply.
  • If the chatbot's AI can't answer a question, the visitor is told once that your team will reply, and the chatbot keeps answering what it can.
  • Either way, a note in the inbox conversation says why it was handed over, the conversation is marked unread, and the assigned teammate (or your owners) get one email.
  • Reply from the inbox. The visitor sees your reply in the chat within a few seconds while they're on your website.
  • If we have no way to reach the visitor, the chat asks for their name and email in case they leave.
  • When a person has taken over, the chatbot stays paused in that conversation. Turn automations back on from the conversation's details when you're done.
  • Closing the conversation shows the visitor that the chat has ended. If they write again, it reopens.

See what it brings in

A chatbot's Analytics page shows, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days:

  • How many chats loaded on a page, were opened, got a message, left an email or phone, became leads, were qualified and paid an order. Leads and orders count only when they came from the chat's own conversation; you mark orders paid yourself.
  • How many chats were handed to your team, why, how long the first reply from a person typically took, and what they became.
  • The latest questions its AI couldn't answer, so you can add the answers to your Knowledge.

What is stored

  • A chat that the visitor never writes in keeps only a random key and times, and is deleted after 30 days.
  • Once a visitor writes, the chat becomes a contact and a conversation in your inbox, like a message on any other channel. Details a visitor types (name, email, phone) are saved on their own contact and are never merged into another customer automatically.
  • For the install check, the chatbot keeps which of your listed websites it loaded on and when. Nothing about the visitor's browsing is kept.

Common problems

The chat button doesn't appear

  • Check the website is in the chatbot's list exactly as visitors open it, including www. if they use it. The chatbot's page names any address it was refused on.
  • Check the chatbot is switched on in its Settings.
  • Check the code is on the page you opened, before </body>.

It answers with "someone from the team will reply" to everything

AI isn't answering: it may be off for your business in Settings → AI, the daily limit may be used up, or your Knowledge doesn't cover the question. The chatbot's page says which under How it answers.

"Fix this first" when turning it on

The conversation has a step that needs attention, for example an AI answer step with nowhere to go when it can't answer. Click Edit messages and steps; the editor marks the step.

Website chat depends on your plan. If Chatbots shows as locked, your plan doesn't include it.
