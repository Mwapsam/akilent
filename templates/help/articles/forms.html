A form collects details inside a conversation: a name, a size, a delivery date, a budget. Every answer is checked before it's saved. Find them under Forms.

Make a form

  1. Add your questions. Choose an answer type for each: short or long text, email, phone, number, one choice, several choices, a date, or a yes/no agreement.
  2. Choose where each answer is saved: the customer's name or email, one of your customer or lead fields, or just with the form's answers.
  3. Write what the customer sees: the message that opens the form, its button, and the thank-you.
  4. Press Make it ready to send.

The preview shows the form as the customer will see it. Mark a question Start a new page here to split a long form into pages.

On WhatsApp: a native form

Set How it's sent to “As a native form” and press Publish to WhatsApp. Customers then fill it in right inside WhatsApp. If you change the questions, press Publish changes; until then customers get the last published version.

On WhatsApp, question labels are short: 20 characters for text and drop-down questions, 30 for choices, 40 for dates. Put any extra explanation in the hint. The form page warns you before you publish.

On Instagram and other channels the same form is asked one question per message, with the same checks.

Send it

  • From a conversation: Start a form in the side panel.
  • From an automation: the Send a form step waits until they finish (or the time limit runs out), then carries on. Later steps can use the answers.
  • To customers who haven't messaged in 24 hours: create a WhatsApp message with an Open a form button and send it, alone or in a campaign.

A form nobody finishes is let go after its time limit (3 days unless you change it), so the conversation is free again. You can also stop waiting for it from the side panel.

Answers

Each form's Answers page lists everyone who got it and what they answered. Download them as a CSV. You can also set a form to track whoever finishes it as an interested customer, and start automations “When a customer finishes a form”.
