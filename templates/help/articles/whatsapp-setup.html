Set up WhatsApp from WhatsApp → Numbers. The page always shows your next step; you're done when a number is registered and a test message is sent.

The steps

  1. Connect with WhatsApp — sign in with Meta and pick or create your WhatsApp Business Account and phone number.
  2. Registration — we register the number on the WhatsApp Cloud API automatically. If it fails, click Retry registration; you don't need to sign in with Meta again.
  3. Send a test message — enter your own phone number and we'll send a WhatsApp test message. You don't have to reply for it to count.
  4. Confirm incoming messages (recommended) — reply to the test message. This proves messages from customers reach you.

What you need from Meta

  • Business Verification completed in Meta Business Manager.
  • App Review approval for whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management.
  • A phone number that isn't already on WhatsApp, or Meta's free test number for a first run.

Replying in the inbox

  • Messages customers send to your number arrive in your inbox. Reply with text, photos or voice notes.
  • WhatsApp only allows free-form replies within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, the conversation shows that the window is closed, and you can send an approved template instead.
  • Everyone who messages you becomes a contact.

AI replies

Turn AI on in Settings → AI. It works the same on WhatsApp as on your other channels.

  • AI answers as your business, only from what you've told it: your notes, your profile answers and your knowledge base. Add your common questions and their answers there.
  • By default AI only suggests a reply, and someone on your team presses Send.
  • If you choose automatic replies, AI answers the kinds of question you tick on its own. To let it answer from your knowledge base, tick Questions your knowledge base answers.
  • When AI can't answer, it tells the customer once that your team will reply and leaves the conversation for you. You can change that message or switch it off.
  • Complaints, refunds, cancellations and disputes always go to a person.

Templates

WhatsApp only lets businesses start a conversation, or write after the 24-hour window, with a template Meta has approved.

  • Create one under Templates → WhatsApp. Meta reviews it, and it can be used once it's approved.
  • If you made templates in Meta Business Manager, sync them to bring them in.

Campaigns

Send an approved template to many contacts at once from New WhatsApp campaign. Customers who replied STOP don't receive it.

One-time codes

Your own system can send login and confirmation codes to customers on WhatsApp through the API. Set it up on WhatsApp → Codes; the developer guide is WhatsApp one-time codes.

Common problems

"WhatsApp Business Account not found"

Meta didn't give us access to a WhatsApp Business Account. Connect again and select or create one during sign-in.

"Your WhatsApp Business Account has no phone number"

Add a phone number to the account in Meta Business Manager, then connect again.

Registration failed (error 133010)

The number isn't registered on the Cloud API. Click Retry registration. If it keeps failing, check that the number isn't registered with another provider, then reconnect.

Access token rejected / credentials not working

The token expired or was revoked. Use Connect with WhatsApp to reconnect and refresh it.

The test message failed

  • Check the number has WhatsApp and includes the country code.
  • With a Meta test number, the recipient must be added as a test recipient in Meta first.
  • If the message says the test template isn't available, contact support.

No incoming messages

Reply to the test message from your phone. If "Last webhook received" still shows nothing, ask your administrator to check the webhook setup in Meta.

Sends that fail repeatedly turn the number to Attention needed; a successful send clears it.
