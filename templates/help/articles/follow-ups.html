A follow-up is work for your team: a customer someone needs to get back to. Only your team sees it. Nothing is sent to the customer, not when you set it and not when it's due. To message the customer, reply in the chat as usual.

Older versions of Akilent called these "reminders". They are the same thing.

Set one

  • In a chat, press Follow up tomorrow under the message box (tomorrow at 9:00 your time).
  • For another time or a note for your team, use the Follow-up section of the chat's side panel, or the Follow-ups card on a customer's page or an interested customer's page. A customer with no chat yet can have one too; it opens their customer page.
  • When AI suggests one, it shows as Suggested follow-up. Nothing happens until someone presses it, and it is still for your team only.
  • Automations can set one with the Set a follow-up for your team step, for whoever is assigned to the chat, the whole team, or a teammate.

A chat keeps one follow-up of each kind. Setting another on the same chat keeps the one already there: an earlier time replaces a later one, and a new note is added to its note on a line of its own.

Times are in your business's timezone, the one set with your opening hours.

The Follow-ups page

The Follow-ups page lists each customer once, in three parts:

  • Waiting for your reply: the customer wrote and nobody has answered for a day. Replying in the chat clears it.
  • Due: due now or late.
  • Coming up: due later.

Mine shows follow-ups given to you and those for the whole team; Whole team shows everyone's. The number beside Follow-ups in the menu is the Mine count. The dashboard and Business Health count the whole team, and say so.

When it's due

It moves to Due and the count goes up. If it is given to you and Email me is on (on the Follow-ups page), you get an email, at most once an hour. Whole-team follow-ups and customers waiting for a reply aren't emailed. Your team may get an email about it; the customer never does.

Done, Later, Not needed

  • Done when it's handled. Your Insights show how many were done, and how many on time.
  • Later moves it to in 1 hour, tomorrow morning, next week, or a date you pick.
  • Under More: Give to… a teammate or the whole team, Edit note, or Not needed, which cancels it and keeps it in the history.

Set by Akilent

When a customer has waited a day without a reply, Akilent adds a Waiting for your reply follow-up. It closes itself when anyone on your team replies in that chat. Follow-ups a person set stay open until someone marks them done; if the customer writes again, the side panel asks whether it's still needed.
