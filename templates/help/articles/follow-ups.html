A follow-up is a reminder for your team to get back to a customer. It never sends anything to the customer.

Set one

  • In a conversation, press Remind me tomorrow under the message box (tomorrow at 9:00 your time).
  • For another time or a note, open the side panel's Follow-up section.
  • Automations can add one with the Remind your team to follow up step.

Times are in your business's timezone, the one set with your opening hours.

When it's due

Due reminders show on the Follow-ups page, with a count next to Follow-ups in the menu. You'll also see a message on screen. The person whose list it's on gets an email, at most once an hour; turn these emails off on the Follow-ups page.

Snooze, pass on, done

  • Snooze it for an hour, three hours, tomorrow morning, next week, or a time you pick.
  • Under More, move it to a teammate's list, change the note, or remove it.
  • Done when you've followed up. Your Insights show how many you completed, and how many on time.

Automatic reminders

If a customer has waited a day without a reply, Akilent adds a Waiting for a reply reminder. These close themselves when anyone on your team replies in that conversation. Reminders a person set stay open until someone marks them done; if the customer writes again, the side panel asks whether it's still needed.
