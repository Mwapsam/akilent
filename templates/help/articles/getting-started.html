Welcome! This guide gets your workspace ready and then follows one customer from a post to a paid order. Your setup checklist on the Setup page shows the same steps for the channels you picked when you signed up.

Step 1: Connect your channels

Connect the channels your customers already use. You can add the others later.

    {% if "whatsapp" in enabled_channels %}
  • WhatsApp: connect your business number through Meta's guided setup. See Connect & use WhatsApp.
    • {% endif %} {% if "instagram" in enabled_channels %}
  • Instagram: connect your business account so its DMs arrive in the inbox. See Connect & use Instagram.
    • {% endif %} {% if "facebook" in enabled_channels %}
  • Facebook Page: connect the Page you post to, for publishing from Content Studio. See Connect your Facebook Page.
    • {% endif %} {% if "email" in enabled_channels %}
  • Email: add the domain you send from and its DNS records. See Add & configure a sending domain.
    • {% endif %}

Step 2: Tell Akilent about your business

Set your business hours so automatic replies know when you're closed, and import your customers from Customers. If you use AI, teach it your answers under Settings → AI.

Step 3: Follow one customer through

    {% if "instagram" in enabled_channels or "facebook" in enabled_channels %}
  1. Attract: write a post in Content Studio{% if "whatsapp" in enabled_channels %} and add a WhatsApp chat link{% endif %}. See Plan and publish posts in Content Studio.
    2. {% endif %} {% if "whatsapp" in enabled_channels or "instagram" in enabled_channels %}
  2. Engage: answer the customer from the shared inbox. Assign the chat to a teammate if someone else should handle it.
    3. {% endif %}
  3. Convert: mark them as interested, set a follow-up, then record the order and send a payment link.
  4. Measure: see what your conversations turned into on Insights{% if "whatsapp" in enabled_channels %}, and on each post, the chats, leads and paid orders it brought in (how it's counted){% endif %}.

Step 4: Invite your team

Bring colleagues into your workspace from Settings → Team. See Invite & manage your team.

Your progress is tracked on your Setup page, and a suggested next step appears in the inbox once you're up and running.
