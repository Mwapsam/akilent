Connect your Facebook Page so Content Studio can publish and schedule posts on it. Facebook in Akilent is for publishing: messages sent to your Page (Messenger) don't come into the inbox.

Before you start

  • You need to be an owner or admin of the Akilent workspace.
  • On Facebook, you need to manage the Page you want to post to.

Connect a Page

  1. In Content Studio, open the Channels tab.
  2. Under Facebook Page, click Connect Facebook Page.
  3. Sign in to Facebook and allow Akilent to post to your Pages.
  4. If you manage more than one Page, pick the one to post to.

The Channels tab now shows the Page as Connected, and Facebook appears under Publish to when you write a post.

"Reconnect needed"

Facebook sometimes stops accepting the saved access, for example after a password change or when a permission is removed. The Channels tab then says Reconnect needed, and Facebook posts fail until you reconnect. Click Connect Facebook Page again to fix it.

Disconnect

On the Channels tab, click Disconnect. Posts already on your Page stay there. Facebook posts can't be published until a Page is connected again.
