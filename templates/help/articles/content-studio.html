Content Studio is where you plan, write and publish posts for Instagram and your Facebook Page. You write a post once, adjust it for each channel when you need to, and choose when each one goes out.

Before you start

Connect somewhere to publish to. On the Content Studio Channels tab you can see what's connected:

  • Instagram: see Connect & use Instagram.
  • Facebook Page: see Connect your Facebook Page.

You can write and save posts before anything is connected. A channel only needs to be connected when the post is published.

Plan your content

The Planning tab holds post ideas before they're written. Group them by content pillar (the themes you post about, such as "New arrivals") and by campaign (a promotion with start and end dates). The Calendar tab shows scheduled and published posts by week, with unscheduled drafts underneath.

Write a post

  1. Click New post.
  2. Under Publish to, tick Instagram, Facebook or both.
  3. Write the caption once under All channels. Switch to a channel's tab to give it its own version, for example a shorter caption for Instagram.
  4. Add your image or video and pick the format. The checklist at the bottom tells you if something won't work on a channel, such as a caption that's too long.
  5. Optional: on a new post, Start with AI takes a short description and offers three captions to choose from. Nothing changes until you pick one.

Drafts save automatically as you type.

Publish or schedule

Publish now, or pick a date and time. To post on each channel at a different time, schedule the channels separately. The post page shows each channel's status, so you can see that Facebook published even if Instagram is still waiting.

Review before publishing (optional)

An owner or admin can switch on Require review on the Channels tab. Posts then need an owner's or admin's approval before they can go out. If someone edits an approved post, it needs approving again.

See what a post brought in

The post page shows reach and engagement from Instagram and Facebook. Add a WhatsApp chat link to see the chats, leads and paid orders each post brings: see Track which posts bring WhatsApp chats.
