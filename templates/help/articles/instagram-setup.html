Set up Instagram from Instagram. Once an account is connected, its direct messages arrive in your inbox and you can add comment rules.

Connect your account

  1. Click Connect Instagram and sign in with the Instagram account you use for your business. It has to be a professional account.
  2. Approve the permissions Meta asks for.
  3. If you manage more than one account, pick the one to connect.

The account shows as ready when it's connected. You can connect more than one account.

Direct messages

  • Messages customers send you on Instagram arrive in your inbox, next to your other conversations. Photos, videos and voice notes come through too.
  • Reply from the inbox. You can send text, photos and voice notes.
  • Instagram only lets businesses reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, the conversation shows that the window is closed, and Instagram won't deliver a reply until the customer messages again.
  • Everyone who messages you becomes a contact.

AI replies

Turn AI on in Settings → AI. It works the same on Instagram as on your other channels.

  • AI answers as your business, only from what you've told it: your notes, your profile answers and your knowledge base. Add your common questions and their answers there.
  • By default AI only suggests a reply, and someone on your team presses Send.
  • If you choose automatic replies, AI answers the kinds of question you tick on its own. To let it answer from your knowledge base, tick Questions your knowledge base answers.
  • When AI can't answer, it tells the customer once that your team will reply and leaves the conversation for you. You can change that message or switch it off.
  • Complaints, refunds, cancellations and disputes always go to a person.

Comment rules

Set these up on Instagram → Comment rules.

  • Automatic private replies send one DM to someone who comments on your posts. A rule can match a keyword, comments that sound like someone wants to buy, or any comment. Write the message yourself; {username} is replaced with their name. When they reply, the conversation lands in your inbox. Each comment gets at most one automatic DM. If several rules match, only the first one in order replies.
  • Moderation rules hide or delete comments that look like spam or abuse, or that match your keywords. They can also flag a comment for your team to review.

Each rule can be switched off without deleting it.

Common problems

"No Instagram Business Account found"

The account you signed in with isn't a professional account. Switch it to a professional account in the Instagram app, then connect again.

"Your connection request expired" or "Instagram setup wasn't completed"

The sign-in link only works once and for a short time, or the Meta window was closed early. Click Connect Instagram to start again.

"Connection expired"

Instagram stopped accepting the connection, so messages can't be sent or received. Click Reconnect. Your conversations are kept.

Comments don't get an automatic reply

  • Check the rule is switched on and that the comment matches it.
  • Each comment gets one automatic DM at most, so replies under the same comment won't send another.
  • Check the account isn't showing Connection expired.
Disconnecting an account stops new messages from arriving. Past conversations stay in your inbox.
