An automation does something on its own when a customer messages you, becomes interested, pays, or finishes a form. Find them under Automations.

Start from a ready-made one

The Automations page groups ready-made automations by what you want help with: answering questions, never missing a customer, and keeping your team informed. Pick one, fill in your words and turn it on. Each card tells you if something is missing first, like a connected channel or an approved WhatsApp message.

Want something specific? Use Build to describe it, or Build your own to start from a blank page.

Build your own

  1. Choose when it starts, for example “When a customer messages you”, and, for messages, the words it listens for.
  2. Press + to add what happens next: send a reply, ask with buttons, send a form, wait, check something, tag the customer, tell your team, or remind your team to follow up.
  3. Steps that can go more than one way (a choice, a check, a form) show where each answer leads. The Map tab shows the whole path.

Your work saves as you go, and problems show next to the step they're in. In plain words above the steps says what the automation will do.

Test it before customers do

Press Test it, type what a customer might say, and see exactly what they'd get back. Answer its questions as the customer would. Testing sends nothing and changes no records, even when you pretend to be a real customer.

Changing one that's on

Editing an automation that's on doesn't change it straight away. Your edits wait as unpublished changes until you press Publish changes. Customers already part-way through finish on the version they started with.

Only owners and admins can turn automations on or off.

Pause, archive, and one conversation at a time

  • Pause: nothing new starts, and customers part-way through are held. When you turn it back on, they carry on with the time they had left. Choose Turn on fresh to let them go instead.
  • Archive: off for good. Customers part-way through are let go.
  • In a conversation's side panel you can pause automations in that conversation, stop one for that customer, or run one by hand. Under Automations you can also make them step back for a few hours whenever a teammate replies.

When something goes wrong

Open View activity on an automation to see every customer who went through it. Open one to see each step in order, and, for a failed run, Try again or Skip this step. If an automation fails, owners get an email, at most once a day per automation.

Free-text replies and buttons can only reach a WhatsApp customer within 24 hours of their last message. For later messages, add an approved WhatsApp message as the step's fallback, or use a “Send a WhatsApp message” step.

Wondering why a customer didn't get a reply? Why didn't it reply? explains what each automation did with their latest message.

Only one automation answers any one message, so a customer never gets two replies. If two would answer, the one with the lower priority number goes first (turn on Advanced in the editor to set it).
